How does Base64 work?

Type a word and work through every step yourself — from ASCII values to binary bits to the final encoded string.

Step 1
Choose your word
Lowercase letters only. Try something short to start — "hack" is a classic.
>_ 0 / 16
Step 2
ASCII decimal lookup
Every character maps to a number. These are the decimal ASCII values for each letter in your word.
QUICK REFERENCE — lowercase a–z
Step 3
Convert each ASCII value to 8-bit binary
Each bit position is a power of 2: 128 · 64 · 32 · 16 · 8 · 4 · 2 · 1. Click bits to toggle them, or hit solve to reveal a row.
1286432168421
Step 4
Join all bits, then split into groups of 6
Concatenate every binary value into one long stream, then rechunk into 6-bit groups for base64 encoding.
Step 5
Decode each 6-bit group to a decimal value
Same idea, but now with only 6 bit positions: 32 · 16 · 8 · 4 · 2 · 1. Each gives you a number 0–63.
32168421
Step 6
Look up each value in the Base64 alphabet
0–25 maps to A–Z, 26–51 to a–z, 52–61 to 0–9, 62 = +, 63 = /
